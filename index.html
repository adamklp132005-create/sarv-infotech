About Mahatma Gandhi

Mahatma Gandhi, popularly known as the Father of the Nation, was one of India's greatest freedom fighters. He was born on 2 October 1869 in Porbandar, Gujarat.

Gandhi believed in truth, non-violence and peaceful resistance. He played a major role in India's struggle for independence from British rule.

His Admirable Qualities

  • Truth: He always encouraged people to follow truth.
  • Non-Violence: He believed that change could be achieved peacefully.
  • Leadership: He inspired millions of Indians.
  • Simple Living: He lived a simple and disciplined life.
  • Equality: He worked against discrimination and social inequality.

Important Events

  • 1869: Born in Porbandar, Gujarat.
  • 1893: Went to South Africa and fought against racial discrimination.
  • 1915: Returned to India.
  • 1930: Led the famous Salt March.
  • 1942: Started the Quit India Movement.
  • 1947: India achieved independence.
  • 1948: Passed away on 30 January.

Famous Message

"You must be the change you wish to see in the world."

Legacy

Gandhi's philosophy of truth and non-violence influenced freedom movements and leaders around the world. His life continues to inspire people to choose peace, kindness, equality and courage.
